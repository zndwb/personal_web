About

I am a Master of Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (2026–2028). I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from China Agricultural University in 2026.

My research is on machine unlearning for large language models, including Mixture-of-Experts models and self-evolving LLM agents, advised by Prof. Ren Wang at Illinois Institute of Technology. I have also worked on diffusion models, graph learning, and reinforcement learning, both in research and in industry internships.

Seeking: Machine Learning Engineer internship (Summer 2027) and co-op (Fall 2027).
