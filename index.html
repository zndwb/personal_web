Research

Illinois Institute of Technology

Jul 2025 – Present
Research Intern · Advisor: Prof. Ren Wang · Remote
  • MoE unlearning (first author, under review at IEEE TPAMI). Showed that single-expert unlearning in Mixture-of-Experts LLMs is superficial: the knowledge comes back after brief fine-tuning. Proposed PLEU, a progressive layer-wise expert erasure method. On DeepSeek-V2-Lite and Qwen1.5-MoE it had lower knowledge recovery than single-expert baselines in every setting, preserved MMLU utility, and ran 67–85% faster than mode-connectivity unlearning.
  • Agent unlearning. Core contributor to unlearning for self-evolving LLM agents, which removes knowledge stored in agent memory and experience, not only in weights. Designed the experimental setup and ran experiments.
